# Luxury Bathtub Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

> *Synthetic Demo Knowledge Document for CraftFlow Development*

### Q1: How heavy is a natural stone bathtub and can my floor support it?
**Answer:** Our solid stone bathtubs weigh between 600 kg and 800 kg empty, and up to 1,100 kg when filled with water and an adult occupant. Concrete subfloors generally support this load without modification. Timber subfloors require a structural engineer's assessment and floor joist reinforcement to support at least 4.5 to 5.0 kN/m² concentrated load.

### Q2: Does marble get cold quickly when taking a bath?
**Answer:** Marble is a natural thermal conductor with high thermal mass. Initially, the surface may feel cool to the touch. However, once hot water is introduced, the solid stone absorbs the heat and radiates warmth throughout the soaking session, retaining bath temperature substantially longer than fiberglass or thin acrylic tubs.

### Q3: How do I clean and maintain a marble bathtub?
**Answer:** Clean exclusively with warm water and a pH-neutral liquid stone soap or microfiber cloth. Never use bleach, vinegar, ammonia, descalers, acidic citrus cleansers, or abrasive scouring pads, as acid etches calcium carbonate instantly. We recommend re-applying our food-safe penetrating stone sealer every 12 months.

### Q4: Are custom sizes and stone types available?
**Answer:** Yes. Approximately 60% of our commissions are bespoke. We can adjust length, width, depth, rim bevels, and drainage locations to exact architectural specifications. We also source specialty marble varieties including Arabescato, Portoro, and Onyx upon request.

### Q5: What is the typical lead time for a custom bathtub?
**Answer:** Standard catalog bathtubs have a lead time of 8 to 12 weeks. Fully customized commissions requiring quarry block sourcing, photogrammetric 3D slab approvals, and bespoke carving take 12 to 16 weeks from technical sign-off and deposit.

### Q6: How is the bathtub shipped and delivered?
**Answer:** Each bathtub is secured within an ISPM-15 heat-treated, fumigated timber crate with high-density EVA shock-dampening foam. Shipments are transported via air freight or ocean container with full transit insurance. Delivery is curbside or white-glove inside delivery by specialized stone riggers.
