# Care & Maintenance Guide for Marble Bathtubs

> *Synthetic Demo Knowledge Document for CraftFlow Development*

## Protecting Your Natural Stone Bathtub

Natural marble is composed primarily of calcium carbonate, making it sensitive to acidic substances and abrasive cleaning techniques. Proper care ensures the stone develops a magnificent patina and retains its lustre for generations.

### Daily and Weekly Cleaning Routine
1. **Rinse Thoroughly:** After each bath, rinse the basin walls with warm clean water to remove soap scum, body washes, and bath salts.
2. **Microfiber Wipe Down:** Dry the surface with a plush microfiber towel or soft squeegee. Removing standing water prevents mineral and hard water deposits.
3. **Approved Cleaners:** Use only pH-neutral liquid stone soap (pH 7.0) diluted in warm water. Avoid multi-surface sprays, dish detergents, and glass cleaners.

### Forbidden Substances — Absolute Zero Tolerance
- **Acids:** Vinegar, lemon juice, citric acid, salicylic acid, and glycolic facial washes etch marble surfaces on contact.
- **Bleach & Ammonia:** Highly alkaline cleaners strip protective penetrating sealers and dull the surface finish.
- **Abrasives:** Scouring powders, magic erasers, and steel wool create microscopic scratches that harbor dirt.
- **Essential Oils & Bath Bombs:** Highly pigmented bath bombs and concentrated essential oils may penetrate micro-fissures and cause deep stains.

### Sealing Recommendations
Every CraftFlow bathtub is pre-treated at our atelier with three coats of breathable fluoropolymer penetrating sealer.
- **Residential Frequency:** Reseal every 12 to 18 months depending on bath frequency and water hardness.
- **Water Drop Test:** Sprinkle a few droplets of water onto the dry bathtub rim. If the water beads tightly after 10 minutes, the seal is intact. If the stone darkens and absorbs the water, immediate resealing is necessary.

### Etch and Scratch Restoration
Light surface etching (loss of sheen caused by contact with acidic products) can be buffed out using our Marble Polishing Cream and a white nylon pad. Deep scratches or chips require an on-site master stonemason polishing service.
