# International Orders & Customs Guide

> *Synthetic Demo Knowledge Document for CraftFlow Development*

## Global Orders & Export Compliance

CraftFlow delivers bespoke natural stone bathtubs worldwide, serving private estates, architects, luxury hotels, and yacht fitters across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

### Export Documentation & Compliance
All international export crates comply strictly with:
- **ISPM-15 Phytosanitary Certification:** Heat-treated timber crates marked with official IPPC stamps to satisfy customs biosecurity in the USA, Canada, UK, EU, Australia, and GCC.
- **Customs Valuation & Commercial Invoices:** Accompanied by HS Code 6802.91 (Worked Monumental or Building Stone - Marble/Travertine).
- **Certificate of Origin:** Authenticating Italian, Spanish, or European quarry origins.

### Incoterms Offered
1. **DAP (Delivered at Place):** Delivered to the client's residential curb or commercial jobsite. Client or their local customs broker is responsible for import VAT, GST, and local customs duties.
2. **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid):** CraftFlow manages all import clearances, duties, and port taxes upfront. Available for United States, United Kingdom, and European Union destinations.
3. **CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight):** Delivered to the destination seaport with comprehensive maritime insurance.
