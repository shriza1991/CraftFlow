# Shipping, Freight & Delivery Policy

> *Synthetic Demo Knowledge Document for CraftFlow Development*

## Global Delivery Standards

Every CraftFlow stone piece is individually crated and shipped under strict white-glove art-transport protocols.

### 1. Crating Specifications
- **Crate Construction:** Certified ISPM-15 heat-treated exterior plywood crates engineered to withstand multi-axis transit forces.
- **Internal Cushioning:** Laser-cut cross-linked EVA closed-cell foam contoured to the specific curvature of the tub.
- **Sensors:** Every crate is fitted with tamper-evident ShockWatch impact sensors and TiltWatch inclination indicators.

### 2. Shipping Methods & Transit Times
- **Ocean Freight (Standard):** 4 to 6 weeks door-to-port or door-to-door, depending on international destination.
- **Air Freight (Expedited):** 7 to 10 business days airport-to-airport. Subject to heavy-cargo aircraft availability.
- **Domestic Road Transport:** Dedicated air-ride suspension flatbed or curtain-sided logistics trucks with tail-lift equipment.

### 3. Delivery Options
1. **Curbside Delivery (Standard):** Hydraulic liftgate delivery to the property driveway or loading bay. Client is responsible for indoor rigging.
2. **White-Glove Rigging & Positioning:** Specialized stone-rigging crews transport the crate indoors, uncrate, inspect, and position the bathtub onto its final bathroom foundation. Requires a site-rigging assessment.

### 4. Transit Insurance & Claims
All shipments include 100% Comprehensive All-Risk Transit Insurance covering total loss, cracking, and damage during transport.
- The receiving contractor or client must inspect the external crate and ShockWatch sensor before signing the bill of lading.
- Any crate damage must be noted on the delivery receipt with photographic evidence taken within 24 hours of delivery.
