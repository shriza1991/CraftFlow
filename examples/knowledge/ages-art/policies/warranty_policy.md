# Limited Warranty Policy

> *Synthetic Demo Knowledge Document for CraftFlow Development*

## Craftsmanship & Structural Warranty

CraftFlow warrants that every solid stone bathtub is free from structural defects in stone selection and artisanal craftsmanship for a period of ten (10) years from the date of final delivery.

### Scope of Coverage
- **Structural Integrity:** Guaranteed against structural cleavage, splitting, or water leaks resulting from inherent material flaws or masonry fabrication defects.
- **Factory Surface Finishes:** Guaranteed for two (2) years against finish delamination or premature degradation under recommended residential use.

### Exclusions & Limitations
The warranty does not cover:
1. **Acid Etching & Chemical Damage:** Surface dulling or pitting caused by contact with acidic cleaning agents, citrus soaps, vinegar, or abrasive powders.
2. **Improper Structural Subfloor Support:** Damage or stress fractures arising from deflection in unreinforced subfloors or uneven foundation support.
3. **Plumbing & Installation Faults:** Overtightened waste fittings or unapproved structural modifications made by third-party contractors.
4. **Natural Geological Variations:** Natural dry veins, calcite crystal pockets, mineral inclusions, color gradations, and fossil lines are intrinsic hallmarks of authentic geological stone and do not constitute defects.
5. **Commercial High-Traffic Use:** Commercial spas and hotels are subject to a separate commercial warranty agreement.
