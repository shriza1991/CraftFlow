The Calacatta Viola Heritage bathtub is an ultra-luxury centerpiece sculpted from Italian Calacatta Viola marble quarried in the Apuan Alps. Renowned for its creamy white backdrop intersected by thick, expressive Cabernet wine-toned and violet-purple brecciated veining, no two Viola tubs are ever identical.

## Bespoke Quarry Block Selection
For every Calacatta Viola order, our team provides 3D photogrammetric scans of 2 to 3 available quarry blocks. Clients and interior designers review the vein density, purple-to-white ratio, and directional flow before cutting begins.

## Maintenance Highlights
Calacatta Viola is a calcitic breccia stone. It requires a pH-neutral cleaner (pH 7.0) and regular penetrating sealant re-application every 12 months in residential applications.
