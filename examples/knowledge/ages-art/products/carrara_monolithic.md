# Carrara Monolithic Marble Bathtub — Product Guide

> *Synthetic Demo Knowledge Document for CraftFlow Development*

## Overview
The Carrara Monolithic is a signature freestanding soaking bathtub carved from a single unbroken block of premium Bianco Carrara marble sourced directly from quarries in Tuscany, Italy. Each piece showcases natural grey veining over a soft white crystalline ground, sculpted by master stonemasons with over three decades of artisanal experience.

## Key Specifications
- **Product Code:** CF-BAT-CAR-01
- **Material:** Authentic Bianco Carrara Grade A Marble
- **Base Dimensions:** 1800 mm (Length) x 850 mm (Width) x 580 mm (Height)
- **Net Dry Weight:** 680 kg (approximately 1,499 lbs)
- **Water Capacity:** 320 Litres
- **Base Price:** $14,500 USD (Bespoke finishes and custom sizing available on request)
- **Standard Lead Time:** 8 to 10 weeks from architectural drawing sign-off

## Architectural & Structural Requirements
Due to the monolithic stone mass of 680 kg dry (and up to 1,000 kg when fully filled with water and an occupant), structural load verification by a certified structural engineer is required prior to upper-floor installation. Joists must be rated for concentrated live floor loads of at least 4.5 kN/m².

## Plumbing & Fixtures
- **Drainage:** Standard 1.5-inch (38 mm) or 2-inch (50 mm) center/side brass pop-up waste assembly.
- **Overflow:** Available with integrated concealed channel overflow or non-overflow configurations for floor-mounted deck fillers.
- **Fixtures:** Compatible with floor-mounted freestanding tub fillers. Wall-mounted faucets require a minimum spout projection of 250 mm.

## Finishes & Options
1. **Honed Matte (Standard):** Velvety smooth, non-reflective surface that minimizes water spot visibility.
2. **Polished Gloss:** High-sheen lustrous polish highlighting high-contrast veining.
3. **Leathered/Brushed:** Texturized tactile surface with subtle surface relief.
