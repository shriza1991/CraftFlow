# Nero Marquina Oval Bathtub — Product Guide

> *Synthetic Demo Knowledge Document for CraftFlow Development*

## Overview
The Nero Marquina Oval bathtub is sculpted from deep black Spanish marble quarried in Markina, Basque Country. Characterized by striking, lightning-like white calcite veins traversing an obsidian-black canvas, the Nero Marquina brings bold drama and architectural presence to high-end bathroom suites.

## Key Specifications
- **Product Code:** CF-BAT-NMQ-02
- **Material:** Authentic Spanish Nero Marquina Marble
- **Base Dimensions:** 1750 mm (Length) x 800 mm (Width) x 560 mm (Height)
- **Net Dry Weight:** 620 kg (approximately 1,366 lbs)
- **Water Capacity:** 290 Litres
- **Base Price:** $16,800 USD
- **Standard Lead Time:** 10 to 12 weeks from deposit and drawing confirmation

## Finishes & Characteristics
- **Honed Satin Finish (Recommended):** Deep matte black with soft tactile feel. Recommended for dark stone to reduce visible water droplet mineral marks.
- **High-Gloss Diamond Polish:** Brilliant mirror-like reflection showcasing dramatic white calcite vein patterns. Requires periodic gentle squeegee drying.

## Installation Notes
- Subfloor must support 620 kg dry weight, scaling to over 910 kg when filled.
- Ground floor or reinforced concrete slab installations are ideal.
- Nero Marquina contains calcite veins that should never be exposed to acidic descalers or vinegar-based solutions.
