## Thermal Characteristics
Serpentinite has exceptional thermal mass. When pre-warmed with hot bathwater for 3 to 5 minutes, the stone holds ambient warmth significantly longer than acrylic, cast iron, or composite resin bathtubs, providing an extended ergonomic soaking experience.
