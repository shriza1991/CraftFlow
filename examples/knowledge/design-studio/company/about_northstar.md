# About Northstar Design Studio

Northstar Design Studio is a boutique interior architecture and spatial consulting studio established in 2021.

## Our Philosophy
We believe thoughtful interior architecture elevates daily living through natural light optimization, clean lines, and honest materials. We specialize in residential modern minimalism, space planning for urban lofts, and executive home offices.

## Studio Details
- **Headquarters**: San Francisco, California.
- **Service Area**: In-person consultations throughout the greater Bay Area; virtual consultations worldwide.
- **Founding Principle**: Accessible architectural guidance with transparent, flat-fee consultation options.
