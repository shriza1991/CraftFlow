# Northstar Design Studio FAQ

Frequently asked questions regarding interior design consultations, virtual planning, and fees.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the cost of an initial consultation?
The initial consultation is billed at a flat rate of $100.00 USD for a 60-minute focused session. If you subsequently retain Northstar for a Full Room Design package, the entire $100 fee is credited toward your package invoice.

### Do you offer remote design consultations?
Yes. Approximately 40% of our residential clients engage us via remote video consultations. Remote clients provide room dimensions and photos in advance, and receive identical digital deliverables including floor plans, 3D renderings, and shoppable product links.

### How many revisions are included in design packages?
The Full Room Design Package includes two full rounds of complimentary revisions during the preliminary concept phase. Additional revision cycles can be requested at our hourly design rate of $150/hr.

### What is your typical project timeline?
Initial consultations are scheduled within 3 to 5 business days. Full Room Design packages require 3 to 5 weeks from contract signing to final drawing package delivery.
