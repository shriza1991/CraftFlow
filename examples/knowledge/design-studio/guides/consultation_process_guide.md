# Design Consultation & Onboarding Guide

Northstar Design Studio follows a structured four-phase client onboarding process to guarantee architectural alignment and predictable project schedules.

## Phase 1: Intake & Discovery (Pre-Session)
Upon completing booking via our portal, clients receive an automated Intake Questionnaire requesting:
- Architectural floor plans, builder drawings, or rough room measurements.
- Existing photographs of the room from four corners in daylight.
- 3 to 5 inspirational visual references (Pinterest, architectural journals, or photo archives).

## Phase 2: The 60-Minute Consultation Session
Held either at the client's property or via remote video studio link. The lead designer and client review structural priorities, lifestyle requirements (pet friendly, acoustics, child safety), and spatial constraints.

## Phase 3: Concept Presentation & Scope Selection
Clients receive the Concept Summary within two business days. If the project requires end-to-end design development, the client selects either the Full Room Design Package or Bespoke Architectural Coordination.

## Phase 4: Procurement & Contractor Hand-off
Northstar coordinates directly with licensed contractors, electricians, and furniture fabricators to verify that finished executions mirror the design specifications.
