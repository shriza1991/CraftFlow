# Booking and Cancellation Policy

Guidelines governing appointment scheduling, cancellations, and retainer deposits for Northstar Design Studio.

## Consultation Cancellations & Rescheduling
- **Notice Period**: Clients may reschedule or cancel an Initial Consultation with at least 24 hours advance written notice without penalty.
- **Late Cancellations**: Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice or missed appointments incur a $50 administrative fee; the remaining $50 remains valid as studio credit for 90 days.
- **Refunds**: Cancellations received with greater than 24 hours notice receive a 100% refund to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

## Design Package Payment Milestones
- **Retainer**: A 50% non-refundable retainer deposit is required upon contract signing to commence schematic design work.
- **Final Balance**: The remaining 50% balance is invoiced upon delivery of final CAD floor plans and 3D renderings, payable prior to contractor handover.
