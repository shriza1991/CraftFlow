# Interior Design Consultation — Service Guide

The Initial Design Consultation is Northstar Design Studio's entry-level advisory service (SKU: DESIGN-CONSULT-01). Designed for homeowners, commercial tenants, and architects seeking expert spatial layout, material palette, and interior styling direction.

## Service Overview
- **Duration**: 60 minutes (in-person on-site or high-definition remote video conference).
- **Standard Fee**: $100.00 USD (flat rate).
- **Lead Time / Availability**: Immediate booking availability; sessions scheduled within 3 to 5 business days of booking.
- **Service Category**: Professional Consulting.

## Consultation Scope & Deliverables
During the 60-minute session, a senior interior architect conducts:
1. **Space Assessment**: Evaluation of existing room architecture, natural light exposure, traffic flow, and functional focal points.
2. **Style Profile Formulation**: Synthesis of client design preferences (e.g., Japandi, Warm Minimalist, Mid-Century Modern, or Industrial).
3. **Color Palette & Material Direction**: Curated selection of wall paints, textiles, wood stains, and stone finishes.
4. **Budget & Scope Estimation**: High-level cost modeling for procurement, trade labor, and project milestones.

## Post-Session Deliverable
Within 48 hours following the consultation, the client receives a **Concept Summary Document** containing:
- Annotated digital mood board with palette swatches.
- Space planning sketches highlighting suggested furniture dimensions.
- Prioritized shopping list and vendor recommendations.
- A credit voucher applying the full $100 consultation fee toward any subsequent Full Room Design Package.
