# Full Room Design Package — Service Guide

The Full Room Design Package is Northstar Design Studio's comprehensive residential and commercial space planning service (SKU: DESIGN-ROOM-02).

## Service Scope
- **Price**: Starting at $2,500.00 USD per room (living rooms, primary suites, executive offices).
- **Timeline / Lead Time**: 3 to 5 weeks from contract execution to final design package presentation.
- **Includes**:
  - Exact 2D CAD furniture layouts and dimensioned floor plans.
  - Photorealistic 3D digital renderings (3 camera angles per room).
  - Detailed lighting, millwork, and electrical placement specifications.
  - Complete procurement list with trade discounts passed directly to client.
- **Revisions**: Two (2) complimentary rounds of design revisions included during the schematic presentation phase.
