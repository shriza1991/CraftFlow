# Bespoke Customization Guide

> *Synthetic Demo Knowledge Document for CraftFlow Development*

## Architectural Commissioning & Custom Dimensions

At CraftFlow, over half of our stone bathtubs are bespoke creations tailored to specific architectural blueprints and private client preferences.

### Customizable Parameters
1. **Length, Width & Depth:** Adjust basin dimensions in 50 mm increments (up to 2,200 mm length and 1,100 mm width).
2. **Rim Profiles:**
   - Knife-edge minimalist bevel (15 mm bevel).
   - Ergonomic rounded bullnose (30 mm radius).
   - Flat architectural deck (50 mm wide rim for deck-mounted fittings).
3. **Internal Ergonomics:** Custom lumbar support angle (110° to 125° incline) and carved headrests.
4. **Waste Outlet Positioning:** Center waste, offset foot waste, or integrated linear channel drain.
5. **Exterior Textures:** Fluted, reeded, chiseled rockface, or bush-hammered artisan treatments.

### Commissioning Process
1. **Consultation & Concept:** Client shares architectural CAD drawings, spatial constraints, and aesthetic goals.
2. **CAD & 3D Render Sign-Off:** Our engineering team provides precise 1:1 shop drawings and digital photorealistic renderings for approval.
3. **Quarry Block Selection:** High-resolution photogrammetry of actual quarry stone blocks provided for client selection.
4. **Sculpting & Milestone Updates:** High-definition video progress reports provided at rough carving, hand-honing, and final pre-crating inspection.
