# Production Lead Times & Schedule Guide

> *Synthetic Demo Knowledge Document for CraftFlow Development*

## Standard & Bespoke Timeline Overview

Each CraftFlow luxury stone bathtub is handcrafted by master stonemasons from unsegmented blocks of mountain marble. Because natural stone carving cannot be rushed without compromising structural integrity, our production schedules adhere to the following timelines:

### 1. Catalog Products (Standard Specifications)
- **Bianco Carrara Monolithic:** 8 to 10 weeks.
- **Nero Marquina Oval:** 10 to 12 weeks.
- **Travertine Fluted Roman:** 10 to 12 weeks.
- **Calacatta Viola Heritage:** 12 to 14 weeks.
- **Verde Alpi Monolith:** 10 to 12 weeks.

### 2. Bespoke Architectural Commissions
- **Shop Drawing & 3D CAD Phase:** 1 to 2 weeks.
- **Quarry Block Extraction & Curation:** 2 to 3 weeks (longer for rare exotic marbles like Onyx or Arabescato).
- **Primary CNC Roughing & Hand Carving:** 4 to 6 weeks.
- **Hand Finishing, Water-Testing & Sealing:** 2 weeks.
- **Crating & Export Documentation:** 1 week.
- **Total Bespoke Timeline:** 12 to 16 weeks prior to freight dispatch.

### 3. Transit Timelines
- **Ocean Container Freight:** 4 to 6 weeks port-to-port.
- **Air Freight Logistics:** 7 to 10 business days airport-to-airport.
