## Natural Stone Selection for Luxury Bathtubs

Selecting the ideal stone variety depends on aesthetic intent, bathroom lighting, and usage patterns. All stones in CraftFlow's catalog are natural geological creations with unique mineral compositions.

### 1. Bianco Carrara Marble (Italy)
- **Geology:** Calcitic metamorphic marble from Carrara, Italy.
- **Visuals:** Warm crystalline white to light silver ground with feathery charcoal-grey veins.
- **Porosity:** Low-to-medium (0.15% to 0.25%).
- **Hardness:** Mohs 3 to 4.
- **Care Profile:** Requires periodic sealing against tannin and oil stains. Excellent resistance to ambient moisture.

### 2. Nero Marquina (Spain)
- **Geology:** Bituminous compact marble from the Basque Country, Spain.
- **Visuals:** Obsidian black matrix traversed by sharp white calcite fissures and fossils.
- **Porosity:** Very low (0.10% to 0.18%).
- **Care Profile:** Shows lime deposits from hard water more readily than white stones. A gentle squeegee after bath use is recommended.

### 3. Roman Travertine (Italy)
- **Geology:** Terrestrial limestone deposited by geothermal springs in Tivoli.
- **Visuals:** Cream, beige, and warm walnut linear sediment banding.
- **Pore Structure:** Naturally porous with vesicular hollows.
- **Finishes:** Resinated interior basin for hygienic bathing; unfilled organic exterior available.

### 4. Calacatta Viola (Italy)
- **Geology:** Brecciated tectonic marble.
- **Visuals:** Dramatic purple, Cabernet, and burgundy clasts cemented in creamy ivory calcite.
- **Exclusivity:** Highly coveted for statement master suites.

### 5. Verde Alpi (Italy)
- **Geology:** Alpine serpentinite.
- **Visuals:** Deep pine and forest green with emerald swirls and white calcite webbing.
- **Thermal Properties:** Outstanding thermal mass and retention.
