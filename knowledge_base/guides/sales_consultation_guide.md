# Sales & Consultation Workflow Guide

> *Synthetic Demo Knowledge Document for CraftFlow Development*

## Guiding High-Net-Worth Clients & Interior Designers

Working with luxury stone bathtub clients requires empathy, architectural precision, and prompt technical clarity.

### 1. Initial Qualification Questions
During the first discovery call or WhatsApp exchange, our sales advisors clarify:
1. **Architectural Stage:** Is this a new construction or bathroom remodel? What is the projected installation date?
2. **Structural Feasibility:** Has the client or contractor consulted a structural engineer regarding the 600 kg to 800 kg dead weight?
3. **Aesthetic Direction:** Which stone palette is favored (White Carrara, Black Marquina, Violet Calacatta, or Travertine)?
4. **Dimensions & Spatial Layout:** What are the bathroom's perimeter dimensions, doorway widths, and elevator access dimensions?

### 2. Sample Presentation & Quarry Block Selection
- Clients receive a complimentary Stone Sample Box containing four 100 x 100 mm hand-honed stone tiles showcasing real veining and finishes.
- Once a deposit is secured, high-resolution multi-angle photographs and 3D photogrammetric scans of 2 to 3 available quarry blocks are presented for vein approval.

### 3. Payment Milestones
- **Deposit:** 50% upon CAD drawing approval and quarry block reservation.
- **Mid-Production:** 30% upon completion of CNC roughing and rough hand-carving approval.
- **Balance:** 20% upon final high-resolution video inspection prior to timber crating.
