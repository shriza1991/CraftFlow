## Overview
The Travertine Fluted Roman bathtub pays homage to classical Roman bathhouse architecture. Hewn from Italian Roman Travertine quarried in Tivoli, this tub features hand-carved vertical fluting across its exterior perimeter and a silky-smooth interior basin.

## Pore Treatment Options
Travertine naturally possesses sedimentary pores and open vacua:
1. **Filled & Honed (Standard):** Pores in the interior basin are filled with color-matched natural mineral resin and honed flush, ensuring hygiene, water retention, and effortless cleaning.
2. **Open Pore Exterior (Aesthetic Option):** Fluted exterior flutes can remain unfilled upon request to showcase raw organic antique texture, while interior wet surfaces remain permanently sealed and filled.
